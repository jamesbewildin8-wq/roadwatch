Base map and camera pins are free and need nothing. Live traffic is proprietary data — no open dataset provides it, so it needs a key from a commercial provider. TomTom's free tier is 50,000 tile requests a day with no card required, which is far more than personal use will touch. Get one at developer.tomtom.com, paste it below.
Stored only in this browser, never in the repo. A key in a static site is readable by anyone who opens it — restrict yours to your Pages domain in the TomTom dashboard, or someone else will spend your quota.
Scores smoothness — harsh braking, acceleration and cornering — not speed. There is no road speed-limit data here, so a speeding figure would be invented. The score appears only after you stop; nothing updates while you drive.
